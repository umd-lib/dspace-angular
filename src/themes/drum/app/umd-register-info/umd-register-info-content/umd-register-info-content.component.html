UMD Affiliates

If you are a University of Maryland affiliate (e.g. email address ends in umd.edu or terpmail.umd.edu), you do not need to create a DRUM account. UMD users should login to DRUM by selecting “UMD Login” and proceeding through CAS authentication.

Visit How to Register as a New User in DRUM: UMD Affiliates for screenshot instructions.

Non-UMD Affiliates

If you are not affiliated with the University of Maryland, please contact drum-help@umd.edu to request a Guest Researcher account be created for you to access DRUM.

For non-UMD-affiliated DRUM users, after your account is activated by the DRUM team, you can login to DRUM by entering your email address and password and selecting "Guest Researcher Login".

Visit How to Register as a New User in DRUM: Non-Affiliates for screenshot instructions.

Only non-affiliated Guest Researcher users: If you forgot your password, please visit the Forgot Password page and enter your email address to receive instructions to reset your password.
